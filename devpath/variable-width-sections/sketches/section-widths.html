Section widths — a geometry sketch

Items are single line, ellipsis, never wrapped. Two sizing models are on the page: Fixed gives every column the same declared width at every viewport, and Proportional lets a column grow with the space available, between a floor and a ceiling. Every number is computed from the real formula, the real SLDS tokens, and the real item chrome out of navigatorItem.css — 66px of padding, border and overflow menu per column before a character is drawn.

Not a Lightning facsimile, deliberately. Judge the geometry and the character counts here; density and native feel are only answerable in a real org.
